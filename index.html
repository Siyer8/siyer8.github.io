01 / Research

Open-Circuit Wind Tunnel

Designed, fabricated, and instrumented a compact wind tunnel for airflow testing.

Scope

Independently developed the tunnel geometry, modular CAD, fan-control test rig, and measurement workflow.

Engineering

Calculated the flow path from pressure and velocity requirements, built an interrupt-driven PWM/RPM test setup, and verified performance using an ATS thermal-anemometer system.

Result

Recorded a peak air velocity of 14.8 m/s with the completed, fully functional wind tunnel. View original measurement photo.

02 / Machining

Single-Piston Engine

A functional engine machined and assembled after simulating its toolpaths in Mastercam.

Process

Machined mating steel and aluminum parts using manual mills, lathes, and drill presses while working to assembly fits and tolerances.

Verification

Created and simulated Mastercam toolpaths to check cutting operations, identify potential collisions, and refine the manufacturing sequence before machining.

03 / Software

AI Engineering Assistant

A locally run workspace for technical questions, project notes, and CAD/code workflows.

Architecture

Built in Python and Streamlit with Ollama for on-device inference, ChromaDB for retrieval, and automatic routing between project, advice, and CAD/code modes.

Workflow

Supports file uploads, searchable chat history, selectable local models, persistent project notes, and cited retrieval from indexed engineering documents.

04 / Concept design

FSAE Steering Wheel

An early concept translating driver-interface and packaging requirements into a CAD assembly.

Constraints

Explored a 6 × 11-inch package with cockpit clearance, driver visibility, an onboard display, four buttons, two dials, paddle shifters, and a commercial quick release.

Design focus

Evaluated control placement, screen visibility, grip geometry, and rear packaging in Onshape as a concept for future vehicle integration.

06 / Robotics

FRC NEO Motor Gearbox

A compact dual-motor gearbox assembly modeled for a FIRST Robotics Competition mechanism.

Design

Modeled the motors, gears, shafts, spacers, and support plates as a complete Onshape assembly.

Packaging

07 / Product design

Split Ergonomic Keyboard

A custom split-keyboard case developed through iterative CAD and 3D-printed prototypes.

Design

Modeled a custom split ergonomic keyboard case in Fusion 360 around the PCB, key layout, and individual hand geometry.

Iteration

Produced multiple 3D-printed versions to refine component fit, wrist support, and typing comfort before assembling the working halves.
